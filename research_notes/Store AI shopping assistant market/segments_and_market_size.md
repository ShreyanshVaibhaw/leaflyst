# AI shopping assistants for small online stores - market size, underserved segments, distribution and pricing (state as of November 2026)

Reading notes for the report writer.
Every figure carries a source-type label in brackets.
The tag "opened" means I read the page itself.
The tag "snippet" means the figure came from a search-result summary and the page was not opened, so treat it as lower confidence.
Research date is 23 November 2026.
Facts older than 2026 are marked with their date.
No merchant forum threads were retrieved, so there are no "forum post" sources in these notes.

## 1. How many online stores exist by platform and size in 2026, how many are small, and what do they spend on apps and tools?

### Takeaway
Store Leads counts about 14.2 million live stores in November 2026, with WooCommerce (4.32 million) and Shopify (3.35 million) holding 54 percent between them, and nearly all of them are small.
Paid tool spend is far thinner than store counts suggest: on Shopify only about 600,000 stores show any detectable paid app spend and only about 65,000 show more than $100 a month.

Revenue tiers and tool spend
- A blog snapshot puts about 90% of Shopify stores under $100K a year, 6-7% at $100K-$999K and about 3% at $1M or more, but it dates from 2025 and its origin could not be verified [blog aggregator, 2025; snippet] - [Popupsmart Shopify snapshot](https://community.popupsmart.com/t/shopify-statistics-global-shopify-snapshot-2026/332)
- In a Storeleads panel of 3,593,277 active Shopify stores pulled on 25 May 2026, detectable paid app spend was: $0 or not tracked 2,994,730 stores (83.3%), $1-$99 a month 533,106 (14.8%), $100-$499 a month 57,475 (1.60%), $500-$999 a month 6,364 (0.18%), $1,000 or more a month 1,602 (0.04%) [finance-advisory firm analysis of independent data, May 2026; opened] - [Eightx app bloat report 2026](https://eightx.co/blog/shopify-app-bloat-report-2026)
- Only 65,441 Shopify stores (1.8%) show detectable app spend above $100 a month [finance-advisory firm analysis, May 2026; opened] - [Eightx app bloat report 2026](https://eightx.co/blog/shopify-app-bloat-report-2026)
- The same analysis estimates detectable Shopify app spend at about $300M a year for the $100-plus bucket and about $550M a year for the whole tracked universe, and retracts an earlier $2.8B figure as ten times too high [finance-advisory firm estimate, May 2026; opened] - [Eightx app bloat report 2026](https://eightx.co/blog/shopify-app-bloat-report-2026)
- Caveat stated by that author: the method prices only detectable paid apps from published plans, misses free native tools and enterprise contracts, and the author guesses $200-$300 a month of hidden spend for a typical store [finance-advisory firm estimate; opened] - [Eightx app bloat report 2026](https://eightx.co/blog/shopify-app-bloat-report-2026)
- Storeleads data shows 11.6M app installs across 2.85M live Shopify stores, about 4.1 apps per store, other sources say about 6, Plus brands run 10-15, and the average cost per paid app is about $58.49 a month [finance-advisory firm analysis citing other sources, May 2026; opened] - [Eightx app bloat report 2026](https://eightx.co/blog/shopify-app-bloat-report-2026)
- Modelled typical Shopify app spend by revenue band: under $1M revenue $50-$300 a month on 3-5 apps; $1M-$5M $1,000-$3,500 a month on 6-9 apps; $5M-$20M $5,000-$15,000 a month on 10-12 apps; $20M-$100M $20,000-$80,000 a month [finance-advisory firm model, June 2026, stated accuracy about plus or minus 25%; opened] - [Eightx app spend by revenue band](https://eightx.co/blog/average-ecommerce-shopify-app-spend-by-revenue-band-2026)
- Among several hundred top-performing Shopify stores, median app spend was about $5,500 a month and the median store ran about 13 apps, which describes large stores only [app vendor data, July 2026, biased sample; opened] - [Koala app cost article](https://koala-apps.io/blog/how-much-shopify-apps-cost/)

### Inferences
- WooCommerce plus Shopify total 7,674,653 stores, or 53.9% of all stores Store Leads tracks, so those two platforms define most of the reachable market.
- Almost every store is small: Shopify Plus domains are 2.5% of Shopify stores (84,179 of 3,353,136), and 47% of Shopify stores and 52% of WooCommerce stores with a product count list fewer than 25 products.
- Catalog size is a better proxy than revenue for who needs an assistant: about 888,000 Shopify stores and about 975,000 WooCommerce stores list 100 or more products (my sums of the Store Leads bands).
- The paying base is much smaller than the store base: about 598,500 Shopify stores (16.7%) show any detectable paid app spend, about 65,400 show more than $100 a month and about 8,000 show more than $500 a month (my sums of the Eightx bands).
- Illustrative arithmetic only, on my assumed 5% penetration: 5% of the 598,500 paying Shopify stores at $40 a month is about $14M a year, and 5% of the 65,400 higher-spend stores at $200 a month is about $8M a year.
- A sub-$1M store's whole app budget ($50-$300 a month) is smaller than the entry price of several existing assistants, which caps what a small store can pay for one tool.
- Store Leads counts move a lot between snapshots (Custom Cart went from about 1.66M in an August 2026 snapshot to 2.19M in November, and Wix has two conflicting figures), so platform counts should be quoted as approximate.

### Gaps
- No public 2026 breakdown of stores by revenue tier for any platform was found; Store Leads sells this data but does not publish it.
- No tool-spend data was found for WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or BigCommerce merchants.
- "Custom Cart" (2.19M) is a catch-all, and no count of headless stores was found.
- BuiltWith figures and platform investor reports (for example Shopify's own merchant count) were not retrieved.
- A widely repeated figure of about $120 a month in average Shopify app spend surfaced in search, but I could not trace it to a survey, and it conflicts with the Eightx bands.

## 2. Which store segments are poorly served by existing assistants, and which product categories need guided selling?

### Takeaway
WooCommerce and self-hosted stores are the clearest structural gap: the largest base, about a million large-catalog stores, heavy presence in non-English Europe and considered-purchase categories, and only fragmented bring-your-own-key plugins or hosted tools at $95 a month and up.
Latin America and Saudi Arabia look better served than expected, because the dominant local platforms (Tiendanube, Nuvemshop, Salla) and Meta now ship their own assistants.

### Cited Findings

WooCommerce and self-hosted
- WooCommerce is the largest platform by store count (4,321,517) yet Store Leads can track only 45 of its apps, which shows how fragmented and hard to measure its plugin channel is [independent data vendor; opened] - [Store Leads WooCommerce report](https://storeleads.app/reports/WooCommerce)
- The official WooCommerce AI Shopping Assistant extension needs the merchant to paste in their own OpenAI API key and pay OpenAI for usage [vendor documentation; snippet] - [WooCommerce AI Shopping Assistant docs](https://woocommerce.com/document/ai-shopping-assistant/)
- A 2026 comparison lists PurioChat at $89 once or $49 a year per site plus the merchant's own API costs, Limb AI Chatbot Pro at $49 a year or $199 lifetime plus API costs, and ChatLab at $18-$360 a month with WooCommerce features locked to the $95 a month Standard plan [vendor claim, the author sells PurioChat, tests dated June and November 2026; opened] - [PureThemes WooCommerce chatbot comparison](https://purethemes.net/best-ai-chatbot-for-woocommerce-comparison/)
- That comparison says hosted SaaS chatbots cost WooCommerce stores $1,140-$4,320 a year, and its test store had only 50 products in 6 categories, so it says nothing about large catalogs [vendor claim; opened] - [PureThemes WooCommerce chatbot comparison](https://purethemes.net/best-ai-chatbot-for-woocommerce-comparison/)
- The WooCommerce base is shrinking, down 10% year-over-year in 2026 Q3 [independent data vendor; opened] - [Store Leads WooCommerce report](https://storeleads.app/reports/WooCommerce)
- WooCommerce is far less US-centric than Shopify: the US holds 12.3% of WooCommerce stores, with Germany, Italy, France, Spain, the Netherlands and Poland each at 91,000-141,000 stores [independent data vendor; opened] - [Store Leads WooCommerce report](https://storeleads.app/reports/WooCommerce)

Headless and custom-built
- Store Leads classes 2,192,242 stores (15.4%) as Custom Cart, and these have no app store to distribute an assistant through [independent data vendor; opened] - [Store Leads all-platform report](https://storeleads.app/reports)
- BigCommerce (36,013 stores) and Magento (98,803 stores) are small by store count [independent data vendor; opened] - [Store Leads all-platform report](https://storeleads.app/reports)

Non-English markets
- Localization is the Shopify app category with the most traction: 58% of localization apps clear 10 or more reviews, against 19% of all apps (81% of all apps have fewer than 10 reviews) [app vendor analysis of public listings, June 2026; opened] - [RevenueHunt state of the Shopify app economy](https://revenuehunt.com/state-of-the-shopify-app-economy/)

Latin America

- A trade headline reports Nuvemshop investing R$55 million in AI to lead conversational commerce [press report; headline only, not opened] - [E-Commerce Brasil](https://www.ecommercebrasil.com.br/noticias/nuvemshop-investe-r-55-milhoes-em-ia-e-lanca-solucao-para-liderar-conversational-commerce)

Middle East

Product categories that need guided selling
- Across Gorgias merchants, the shopping assistant influences 1.9% of online-store GMV overall and 3-4% in vehicles and parts, furniture and outdoor gear, electronics and equipment, with basket lift of half or more in those categories [vendor benchmark on its own customers, June 2026; opened] - [Gorgias research](https://www.gorgias.com/research/ai-shopping-assistants-are-now-officially-influencing-sales)
- In the same data apparel and beauty give the largest absolute influenced revenue at a lower rate, and food and beverage shows low influence with a slightly negative basket uplift [vendor benchmark, June 2026; opened] - [Gorgias research](https://www.gorgias.com/research/ai-shopping-assistants-are-now-officially-influencing-sales)
- About 14% of assistant conversations end in an attributed order within three days, and influenced orders average $181 against $123 for the same store's other orders [vendor benchmark, June 2026; opened] - [Gorgias research](https://www.gorgias.com/research/ai-shopping-assistants-are-now-officially-influencing-sales)
- Gorgias itself says about 80% of that influenced GMV rests on the weakest signal (a conversation followed by an order), that attribution is associative and not causal, and that assisted orders may be refunded more often [vendor benchmark, June 2026; opened] - [Gorgias research](https://www.gorgias.com/research/ai-shopping-assistants-are-now-officially-influencing-sales)
- Smaller brands see the assistant touch a larger share of revenue while larger brands see bigger baskets (no per-band numbers given) [vendor benchmark, June 2026; opened] - [Gorgias research](https://www.gorgias.com/research/ai-shopping-assistants-are-now-officially-influencing-sales)
- In India, top-quartile electronics brands convert at nearly 1.5% on WhatsApp against a 0.4% network average [vendor report on its own network, June 2026; opened] - [GoKwik report coverage](https://www.medianews4u.com/eight-in-ten-whatsapp-orders-placed-during-indias-festive-season-last-year-came-from-new-customers-gokwik-data/)
- A vendor benchmark reports that AI-assisted shopping raises purchase likelihood by about 25%, with no category split [vendor claim, Zoovu 2026 as summarised by another vendor; snippet] - [Retainful benchmark summary](https://www.retainful.com/blog/ai-ecommerce-conversion-benchmark)
- On Shopify, 149 apps match the search "quiz", the top three hold 45% of the category's reviews, and even the leaders have only 422, 193 and 171 reviews [app vendor analysis, the author competes in this category, June 2026; opened] - [RevenueHunt state of the Shopify app economy](https://revenuehunt.com/state-of-the-shopify-app-economy/)

### Inferences
- WooCommerce over-indexes on the categories where assistants show the most influence: Business and Industrial (347,730 stores), Computers (147,828) and Autos and Vehicles (134,563) are large on WooCommerce, while Shopify is dominated by Apparel (912,109).
- WooCommerce merchants are used to $49-$199 one-time or annual licences with their own API key, so willingness to pay a monthly SaaS fee is likely lower than on Shopify.
- The WooCommerce gap is real but comes with costs: a shrinking base, no measurable app-store channel and wide hosting variance to support.
- Latin America on Tiendanube and Saudi Arabia on Salla are being served by the platform itself, and Meta is integrating directly, so a third party there competes with the platform owner.
- Guided selling pays off in considered purchases (auto parts, furniture, electronics, equipment) and looks weak in food and low-priced apparel.
- The low review counts of the Shopify quiz leaders suggest guided selling as a standalone small-store category is small today.

### Gaps
- No independent test of assistant quality in non-English languages was found.
- No figures were found for supplements, skincare or B2B wholesale specifically.
- No evidence was gathered on assistants for Wix, Squarespace or BigCommerce stores.
- No store counts were found for the UAE or other Middle East markets beyond Salla's Saudi base.
- No count of headless stores or of assistants built for them was found.

## 3. India and Southeast Asia: WhatsApp and Instagram commerce, local-language and voice use, price sensitivity

### Takeaway
India has about 358,000 tracked stores (131,000 on Shopify, 120,000 on WooCommerce) and a crowded, cheap WhatsApp-assistant market where AI add-ons sell for roughly $30-$75 a month plus per-message fees.
Southeast Asia's online commerce runs mostly through marketplaces and social platforms, so an assistant embedded in a merchant's own site addresses only a small slice there.

- India has 131,150 Shopify stores, the fifth-largest Shopify country [independent data vendor; opened] - [Store Leads Shopify report](https://storeleads.app/reports/shopify)

- In Gorgias's data the assistant's revenue influence is highest (3-4% of GMV) in vehicles and parts, furniture and outdoor gear, electronics and equipment [vendor benchmark, June 2026; opened] - [Gorgias research](https://www.gorgias.com/research/ai-shopping-assistants-are-now-officially-influencing-sales)

### Inferences
- The $200-plus buyers are stores at roughly $1M-$20M revenue with meaningful traffic, a pool on the order of tens of thousands of Shopify stores, far from the mass small-store market.
- The clearest pain is the pricing unit: session-based and per-resolution fees scale with traffic or ticket volume and not with sales, which makes bills unpredictable for stores with many low-intent visitors.
- A store with 20,000-50,000 monthly sessions faces a jump from $10-$60 apps to $209-$368 a month for Rep AI, and that step is where a cheaper, flat-priced assistant has room.
- Considered-purchase stores (auto parts, furniture, electronics, equipment) are the most likely to see enough return to pay $200 or more, and they are the group Gorgias's own data favours.
- Because most complaints were collected by competitors, this evidence shows that price friction exists and does not show how widespread it is.

### Gaps
- No independent survey of merchant satisfaction with paid assistants was found.
- Merchant forums (Reddit, Shopify Community) were not searched, so there is no first-hand forum evidence.
- The Shopify App Store review pages for Rep AI, Gorgias and Alhena were not opened to count negative reviews.
- Gorgias's own pricing page was not opened, and one snippet suggested flat pricing with no per-resolution fee, which conflicts with the other reports.
- No churn figures for any $200-plus assistant were found.

## 8. Is there measurable demand for voice or image-based shopping help on small stores?

### Takeaway
No measured demand on small stores was found.
Consumer surveys show about a third of shoppers have tried visual or voice search, and the one strong usage signal is voice on an Indian marketplace (Meesho), which is company-reported and not a small-store setting.

### Cited Findings
- 36% of survey respondents have used visual search, the same rate as voice search [marketing publisher survey, date and sample not confirmed; snippet] - [Smart Insights](https://www.smartinsights.com/ecommerce/third-consumers-use-visual-search/)
- An aggregator reports 56.1% of US consumers use voice search and 32% use it to make purchases, without clear primary sources [aggregator, 2026; snippet, unverified] - [Capital One Shopping research](https://capitaloneshopping.com/research/voice-shopping-statistics)
- A vendor blog says about 22% of 16-34 year-olds use visual search to discover or buy, against 17% of 35-54 year-olds and 5% of those 55 and over [vendor claim, no sample stated; snippet] - [Imagga blog](https://imagga.com/blog/?p=4784)
- A June 2026 survey of 6,379 respondents in six countries measured comfort with AI image and voice search tools, but the figures sit behind a paywall [independent survey; snippet, numbers not seen] - [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1662440/consumer-openness-to-visual-and-voice-search-tools/)
- Meesho says voice use rose from about 10% of users to nearly 40% of users with access to its Vaani assistant, with a 22% conversion lift, 79% saying voice simplifies shopping and 62% comfortable transacting by voice [vendor claim by a large Indian marketplace, 2026, sample not disclosed; snippet] - [YourStory on Vaani](https://yourstory.com/2026/03/meesho-launches-ai-shopping-assistant-vaani)
- Gallabox sells one agent that handles voice and chat, aimed at appointment and service businesses [software directory review, August 2026; opened] - [Rework WhatsApp AI agents 2026](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-agents/best-ai-agents-for-whatsapp-2026)
- Rep AI sells Virtual Try-On and Deep Research as an add-on at 10% of its bundle price ($73 a month at 80,000 sessions) [competitor blog quoting vendor pricing, October 2026; opened] - [Eesel on Rep AI pricing](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/rep-ai-pricing)
- Among WooCommerce assistants compared, ChatLab does not accept image input and Limb does not list it [vendor claim by a rival, 2026; opened] - [PureThemes WooCommerce chatbot comparison](https://purethemes.net/best-ai-chatbot-for-woocommerce-comparison/)
- OpenCart's own blog frames the topic as a question, "Voice Commerce and Visual Search: Should Your Online Store Care Yet?" [platform vendor blog; title only, not opened] - [OpenCart blog](https://www.opencart.com/blog/voice-commerce-and-visual-search-should-your-online-store-care-yet?page=5)

### Inferences
- Voice and image input on small stores is a supply-side feature today, sold as an add-on, with no usage data showing shoppers ask for it on independent stores.
- The Meesho result suggests voice matters most for first-time, lower-literacy and local-language shoppers in India, which is a marketplace audience more than an own-site audience.
- Image input is a cheap differentiator on WooCommerce, where rivals lack it, but there is no evidence it changes a merchant's buying decision.
- Voice or image should be treated as a later feature, not as the reason a small merchant buys.

### Gaps
- No data was found on the share of on-site searches or chats on small stores that use voice or images.
- No install or review counts for visual-search apps on Shopify or WooCommerce were gathered.
- No merchant survey asking for voice or image assistance was found.
- The Statista survey numbers could not be read.
- No data on voice shopping in Southeast Asian or regional Indian languages was found.
